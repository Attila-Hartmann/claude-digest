Introducing Claude Meetings

Anthropic • 961,240 views • November 12, 2026

Claude Meetings lets Claude join a Google Meet call as a visible participant — not a bot in the background, but a named attendee that follows the conversation in real time. Teammates can ask it questions aloud or in chat and Claude replies in writing; it also builds docs, whiteboards, or other artifacts during the call itself. After the meeting it generates a full page on claude.ai with notes, a transcript, and everything it created. The feature is rolling out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, with support for additional video-conferencing platforms on the roadmap.
